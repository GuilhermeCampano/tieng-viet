# Example: `vn-tone-family`

Invoke with `/tone-family <syllable>` — the skill shows all tonal variants, ranks them by frequency, and offers a quiz.

**Input:** `/tone-family ma`

---

### ma / mà / má / mả / mã / mạ

| Word | Tone | Meaning | Example | Frequency |
|------|------|---------|---------|-----------|
| ma   | ngang (mid-level) | ghost, spirit | Đừng sợ ma! — Don't be afraid of ghosts! | common |
| mà   | huyền (falling) | but, yet; narrative particle | Tôi muốn đi mà trời mưa. — I want to go but it's raining. | common |
| má   | sắc (rising) | cheek; **mom** (Southern dialect) | Má ơi, con về rồi! — Mom, I'm home! | common |
| mả   | hỏi (dipping) | grave, tomb | Thăm mả ông bà. — Visiting the ancestors' graves. | uncommon |
| mã   | ngã (broken rise) | horse (Hán-Việt 馬); code | Mã hóa dữ liệu. — Encrypt the data. | common |
| mạ   | nặng (heavy fall) | rice seedling; mother (archaic) | Cấy mạ xuống ruộng. — Plant the rice seedlings. | uncommon |

**Key takeaway:** `ma` (ghost) vs `má` (mom) vs `mà` (but) — one wrong tone and you're talking to a ghost instead of your mother.

**Tone confusion alert (Portuguese speakers):** `mả` (hỏi) vs `mã` (ngã) feel identical — hỏi dips before rising, ngã cracks mid-rise. `mà` (huyền) easily confused with `ma` (ngang flat).
