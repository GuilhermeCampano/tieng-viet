**Song:** Con Cò (Traditional Vietnamese folk poem / lullaby — public domain)

---

### Full text

> Con cò mà đi ăn đêm,
> Đậu phải cành mềm lở xuống ao.
> Ông ơi ông vớt tôi nao,
> Tôi có lòng nào ông hãy xáo măng.

---

### Line-by-line breakdown

**Verse — key words**

| Word | Type | Meaning |
|------|------|---------|
| con cò | noun | heron / egret (con = animal classifier) |
| mà | particle | narrative connective — softens the action (not "but" here) |
| đi ăn | verb | to go eat / go feed |
| đêm | noun | night |
| đậu | verb | to perch, land (of birds) |
| phải | verb | to suffer / encounter (misfortune) |
| cành mềm | noun phrase | weak / flimsy branch |
| lở xuống | verb + directional | collapse downward |
| ao | noun | pond |

> *Con cò mà đi ăn đêm,* — The heron goes out to feed at night,
> *Đậu phải cành mềm lở xuống ao.* — lands on a weak branch and falls into the pond.

**Grammar:** `đậu phải` — verb + phải = suffered / met with misfortune. Modern: `gặp phải khó khăn` (ran into difficulties).

---

| Word | Type | Meaning |
|------|------|---------|
| ông ơi | vocative | Sir! (ơi = calling particle) |
| vớt | verb | to scoop / fish out (from water) |
| nao | particle | archaic plea marker — "please" (literary) |
| có lòng nào | phrase | if I have any wicked heart / ill intention |
| hãy | particle | polite imperative — "please do" |
| xáo | verb | to cook / stew |
| măng | noun | bamboo shoots |

> *Ông ơi ông vớt tôi nao,* — Oh sir, please fish me out,
> *Tôi có lòng nào ông hãy xáo măng.* — If I have any ill intent, then cook me with bamboo shoots.

🎯 **The twist:** `xáo măng` (stewed with bamboo shoots) is a real dish. The heron stakes its life as proof of innocence.

### Grammar notes

**`phải` after a verb = misfortune**
`đậu phải cành mềm` = landed on and suffered from a weak branch.
Modern: *Gặp phải mưa lớn.* — Ran into heavy rain.

**`ông ơi ông` — doubled address for pleading**
Repeating intensifies the appeal. Same pattern: *Mẹ ơi mẹ!*

**`hãy` — imperative particle**
Before verb = polite request/command. *Hãy cẩn thận.* — Please be careful.
