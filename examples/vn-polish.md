# Example: `vn-polish`

Write a sentence yourself, then invoke the skill to check it.

**Input sentence:** *Hôm nay tôi đi làm muộn vì tôi ngủ dậy trễ*

---

### 1. What's right
Good use of **vì** for cause, and the time reference **hôm nay** at the front. Natural sentence order.

---

### Pass 1 — Corrections

**Corrected:** Hôm nay tôi đi làm muộn vì dậy trễ.

| Error | Original | Fix | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blended verbs | **ngủ dậy** | **dậy** (or **thức dậy**) | ngủ = to sleep; dậy = to get up — can't combine them; "thức dậy" = wake up and get up |
| Redundant pronoun | vì **tôi** ngủ dậy | vì dậy trễ | same subject — Vietnamese drops the repeated pronoun after vì |

---

### Pass 2 — Natural alternatives

| Sentence | Translation | Nuance |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Hôm nay tôi đi làm muộn vì dậy trễ. | Today I was late to work because I got up late. | Clean, neutral — good everyday Vietnamese |
| Hôm nay tôi bị muộn vì thức dậy không kịp. | Today I was late because I didn't get up in time. | **bị muộn** = suffered being late; **không kịp** = didn't make it in time — more natural in speech |
| Sáng nay tôi dậy trễ nên đi làm không kịp giờ. | This morning I got up late so I couldn't get to work on time. | **nên** (so/therefore) chains cause → result more naturally than vì in spoken Vietnamese |

---

### Key contrast

| | Sentence |
|---|---|
| ❌ | vì tôi ngủ dậy trễ |
| ✅ | vì dậy trễ / vì thức dậy không kịp |

**One-line rule:** drop the repeated subject after **vì** — Vietnamese doesn't restate it; and **thức dậy** = wake up, **ngủ** = sleep, never combine them.
