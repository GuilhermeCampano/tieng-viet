Frequency ratings: **common** / **uncommon** / **rare**, based on corpus frequency (e.g. Subtlex-VN).

| Word | Tone | Meaning | Example | Frequency |
|------|------|---------|---------|-----------|
| đắp  | sắc (short ă) | to cover, to build up (wall), to apply (plaster) | Em đắp chăn khi lạnh. — I cover myself with a blanket when cold. | common |
| đáp  | sắc (long a)  | to land; to answer; to board (transport) — Hán-Việt 答 | Máy bay đáp xuống sân bay. — The plane landed at the airport. | common |
| đạp  | nặng          | to pedal, to kick, to tread | Tôi đạp xe đi làm. — I ride my bike to work. | common |
| đập  | nặng (âp)     | to hit, to beat, to slam shut | Đừng đập cửa! — Don't bang the door! | common |
